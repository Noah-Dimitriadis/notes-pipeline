## Appending a continuation recording

This lecture was recorded in multiple parts. You are given the **existing
notes** already produced for the earlier part(s), the **slide deck** (which
may cover material beyond what the existing notes discuss), and a **new
transcript** covering a further continuation of the *same* class session,
recorded as a separate audio file that starts again from 00:00.

Produce one complete, updated set of notes — not two documents stitched
together. Concretely:

- Preserve everything from the existing notes that is still accurate. Do not
  drop content just because it's already there.
- Add new content the new transcript covers, attributed to the correct slide
  (in the existing `### Slide N — Title` structure) or a new plain heading if
  it doesn't map to a slide, following the same four rules as everywhere else
  in these notes.
- Merge rather than duplicate: update the TL;DR, Exam signals, Gaps in my
  notes, Glossary, Open questions, and Transcription confidence sections to
  reflect the *whole* lecture as now known, not just the new part.
- **Timestamp disambiguation is critical.** The new transcript's timestamps
  restart from 00:00 and refer to a *different audio file* than the existing
  notes' timestamps — never let a reader assume a bare `[MM:SS]` from the new
  material refers to the same recording as the existing notes. Mark every
  claim sourced from the new transcript as `[Part N, MM:SS]`, where N is one
  more than the highest part number already present in the existing notes
  (if the existing notes have no part markers at all, treat them as Part 1
  and label new material Part 2). Leave every timestamp already present in
  the existing notes exactly as it is — do not renumber or re-label them.
